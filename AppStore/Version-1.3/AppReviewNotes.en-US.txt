FrameWink 1.3 improves the setup screen at accessibility text sizes. No account or reviewer credentials are required.

To review the change:
1. In Settings, increase Larger Text to an accessibility size at or above 200%.
2. Launch FrameWink on iPhone or iPad with the bundled sample frame.
3. Confirm the complete setup explanation wraps without truncation and the setup actions remain reachable. On narrow iPhone screens, the setup card scrolls so both actions remain available.

The existing FrameWink Lifetime non-consumable is media.jenny.FrameWink.wallmode. Photos and analysis remain on-device. FrameWink has no account, developer server, ads, analytics SDK, or tracking, and it never edits or deletes originals in Apple Photos.
