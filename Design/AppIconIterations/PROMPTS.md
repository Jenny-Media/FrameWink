# FrameWink app icon iteration prompts

Generated with Codex's built-in image-generation tool on 2026-08-14. The
current production icon was supplied as a brand reference only. The generated
masters were 1,254 × 1,254 opaque PNGs; each candidate also has a normalized,
metadata-stripped 1,024 × 1,024 sRGB deliverable.

## Review

Variant 1 is the strongest production candidate. It preserves the recognizable
FrameWink idea while removing the current icon's scalloped edge, and it has the
clearest sun/frame/wink hierarchy at 32 px. Variant 2 is the most distinctive at
large sizes, but the integrated corner becomes visually busy at 32 px. Variant
3 is calm and extremely simple, but its smaller horizontal mark gives up useful
icon-grid presence.

Variant 1 was selected and copied to the production `AppIcon-1024.png`. It has
the strongest full-size hierarchy and remains the clearest candidate in the
32-pixel comparison. Variants 2 and 3 are retained only as design history.
