FrameWink turns an iPad into a private local photo frame.

Please test:
- Launch and watch the clearly labelled bundled sample reel without a Photos permission prompt.
- Choose a small set of photos with the system picker, wait for local Smart Reel curation, review suggestions, and use Never Show Again.
- Enter Frame Mode, navigate in both directions, pause/resume, change timing and layout, rotate the iPad, and leave Frame Mode.
- Open Unlock Wall Mode, verify the one-time purchase copy, complete or cancel the test purchase, and try Restore Purchases.
- With Wall Mode unlocked, tap Choose Automatic Album, accept or deny Photos access, select an album, review its suggestions, and confirm FrameWink never changes the originals.
- Try Strict Offline with a local and an iCloud-only photo. With automatic refresh on, add/remove an album photo in Photos and return to FrameWink.
- Save and switch between multiple frame configurations, including Automatic Album and the four-photo Mosaic layout.
- With Wall Mode unlocked, review the commissioning checklist and foreground-only dim/blackout schedule wording.
- Delete Imported Photos and Delete Automatic Album Cache; confirm the originals remain in Apple Photos.

Please report the iPad model, iPadOS version, exact steps, and whether the app was foregrounded. Guided Access must be started manually in iPadOS, and FrameWink cannot guarantee relaunch after a restart.
