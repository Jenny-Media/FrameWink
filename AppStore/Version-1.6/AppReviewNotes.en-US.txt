FrameWink 1.6 adds native iPhone Duo support using the iOS 27.1 SDK. The app remains universal for iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 15 as the minimum. No account or reviewer credentials are required. The clearly labelled sample experience starts without a Photos permission prompt.

Review steps:
1. On iPhone Duo, start the sample frame on the outer and fully open inner displays. Pause on a recognizable photo, then open, close, rotate, or resize. Confirm the current photo and paused state remain stable. Resume and check the selected Photo Duration.
2. In Book and Tabletop poses, photos use the region above or to the left of the active division; controls use the region below or to its right and respect the division margins and safe areas. There is no separate Duo mode, permission, or purchase.
3. Open Privacy & Data, Photos in This Frame, Choose What Plays, Frame Settings, and the Lifetime screen. Check native Close/Cancel/Done actions and Larger Text, including both iPad Split View positions.
4. Choose photos through Apple's system picker, review the reel, use Never Show Again, and start the frame. Full PhotoKit authorization is requested only after a verified Lifetime entitlement and an explicit automatic-album choice.
5. Open More > Send Feedback. This opens an editable Apple Mail draft without automatic photo or log attachments. If Mail is unavailable, Copy Email is offered.

Photos and analysis stay on-device. FrameWink has no developer server, account, ads, analytics SDK, or tracking and never changes originals in Apple Photos. The approved Lifetime non-consumable remains media.jenny.FrameWink.wallmode. Guided Access requires manual setup; automatic relaunch after restart is not promised.
