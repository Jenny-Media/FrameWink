FrameWink 1.5 adds native iPhone Duo layouts. No account or reviewer credentials are required. The clearly labelled bundled sample experience starts without a Photos permission prompt.

Review steps:
1. On iPhone Duo, open and fold the display through fully open, Book, Tabletop, and closed poses. The fully open photo frame uses the available window; when a fold is active, photos remain above or left of it and controls remain below or right. No separate Duo setting or additional purchase is required.
2. Pause on a recognizable photo, then open, fold, rotate, or resize the window. Confirm the displayed photo and paused state remain stable, then resume and confirm the selected Photo Duration.
3. Open Privacy & Data, Photos in This Frame, photo import, Frame Settings, and the Lifetime screen. Confirm Close, Cancel, and Done controls remain reachable with Larger Text and in both Split View positions.
4. Confirm normal photo selection, review, playback, and timing on iPhone and iPad. PHPicker shares only selected photos; full PhotoKit access is requested only after a verified Lifetime entitlement and an explicit automatic-album choice.

Photos and analysis remain on-device. FrameWink has no developer server, account, ads, analytics SDK, or tracking, and never changes originals in Apple Photos. Send Feedback is an editable Apple Mail draft with no automatic photos or log attachments; the user chooses whether to send it. If Mail is unavailable, Copy Email is offered. The existing Lifetime non-consumable is media.jenny.FrameWink.wallmode. Guided Access requires manual setup. Automatic relaunch after restart is not promised. iOS/iPadOS 15 remains the minimum supported version. Manual release after approval remains the intended release option.
