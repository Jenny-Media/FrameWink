FrameWink turns an iPad into a private local photo frame.

Please test:
- Launch and watch the clearly labelled bundled sample reel without a Photos permission prompt.
- Choose a small set of photos with the system picker, wait for local Smart Reel curation, review suggestions, and use Never Show Again.
- Start the frame, navigate in both directions, pause/resume, change timing and layout from More, rotate the iPad, and close the frame.
- Open More Frame Features, verify the FrameWink Lifetime one-time purchase copy, complete or cancel the test purchase, and try Restore Purchases.
- With FrameWink Lifetime unlocked, choose an album, accept or deny Photos access, review its suggestions, and confirm FrameWink never changes the originals.
- Use an album containing a local and an iCloud-only photo. Confirm preparation continues without restarting as Apple Photos downloads what is needed. Then add/remove an album photo in Photos and return to FrameWink.
- Change the active frame's source, Automatic/Mosaic layout, and timing; confirm the choices survive relaunch.
- Review the collapsed Mounted iPad Tips and the foreground-only night schedule wording in Frame Settings.
- Delete Imported Photos and Delete Automatic Album Cache; confirm the originals remain in Apple Photos.

Please report the iPad model, iPadOS version, exact steps, and whether the app was foregrounded. Guided Access must be started manually in iPadOS, and FrameWink cannot guarantee relaunch after a restart.
