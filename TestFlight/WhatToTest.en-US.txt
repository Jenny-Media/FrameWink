FrameWink turns an iPhone or iPad into a private local photo frame.

Please test:
- With an automatic album playing, pause on a recognizable photo while the album refreshes or after switching albums. Confirm the visible photo stays fixed until Resume, then the latest reel is applied.
- During automatic album preparation, confirm the status continues to show preparation progress instead of briefly reporting that all photos are ready while syncing is still active.
- Launch and watch the expanded twenty-photo bundled sample reel without a Photos permission prompt. Confirm the new travel and nature photos appear varied and well framed.
- Choose photos with the system picker, confirm the count is shown out of 500, and verify a first Smart Reel becomes playable while a larger import continues. Review suggestions and use Never Show Again.
- Start the frame, navigate in both directions, pause/resume, change Photo Duration from More, rotate the device, and close the frame.
- Open More Frame Features, verify the FrameWink Lifetime one-time purchase copy, complete or cancel the test purchase, and try Restore Purchases.
- With FrameWink Lifetime unlocked, choose an album, accept or deny Photos access, review its suggestions, and confirm FrameWink never changes the originals.
- Use an album containing a local and an iCloud-only photo. Confirm preparation continues without restarting as Apple Photos downloads what is needed. Then add/remove an album photo in Photos and return to FrameWink.
- Change the active frame's source and timing; confirm the choices survive relaunch and automatic composition adapts to the window.
- Watch several paired or multi-photo scenes and confirm FrameWink preserves the important parts of each photo instead of forcing an aggressive crop.
- Review the collapsed Mounted Display Tips and the foreground-only night schedule wording in Frame Settings.
- Open Privacy & Data and check the app photo-data size. Use Free Up Unused Space; confirm it keeps your selected photos, current reel, and chosen album while removing unused downloads. Switch among three albums and back to check cache reuse.
- Use the clearly warned Delete All FrameWink Photos action. Confirm it clears the app's photo data and selection without changing originals in Apple Photos. Check that both storage actions show visible progress.

Please report the device model, OS version, exact steps, and whether the app was foregrounded. Guided Access must be started manually, and FrameWink cannot guarantee relaunch after a restart.
