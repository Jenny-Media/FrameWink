FrameWink 1.5 refines navigation, frame layouts, safe-area spacing, and setup controls on iPhone and iPad. No account or reviewer credentials are required. The clearly labelled bundled sample experience starts without a Photos permission prompt.

Review steps:
1. Launch the sample frame, navigate between photos, pause on a recognizable photo, rotate or resize the window, and resume. Confirm the displayed photo, paused state, and selected Photo Duration remain stable.
2. Open Privacy & Data, Photos in This Frame, photo import, Frame Settings, and the Lifetime screen. Confirm Close, Cancel, and Done controls remain reachable with Larger Text and in both iPad Split View positions.
3. Choose personal photos through the system picker, review suggestions, use Never Show Again, and play the frame. Full PhotoKit access is requested only after a verified Lifetime entitlement and an explicit automatic-album choice.
4. Open More > Send Feedback. This is an editable Apple Mail draft with no automatic photos or log attachments; the user chooses whether to send it. If Mail is unavailable, Copy Email is offered.

Photos and analysis remain on-device. FrameWink has no developer server, account, ads, analytics SDK, or tracking, and never changes originals in Apple Photos. The existing Lifetime non-consumable is media.jenny.FrameWink.wallmode. Guided Access requires manual setup. Automatic relaunch after restart is not promised. iOS/iPadOS 15 remains the minimum supported version. This release uses the stable Xcode 27 SDK and does not include native iPhone Duo fold handling. Manual release after approval remains the intended release option.
